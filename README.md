# Glowora (formerly Barber-Q)

Glowora is a queue and booking management app for barber shops and salons, for both men and women. Customers can find a shop, join the live queue and pay online, while shop owners manage their queue, services, staff and earnings from a dedicated dashboard.

## Features

### For customers

- Browse shops and view shop details and photos
- Join a shop's queue and track their position
- Pay online with Razorpay (cards, UPI) or by scanning the barber's own UPI QR code
- Profile management and a customer support / FAQ section

### For barbers and shop owners

- Live queue management
- Add and manage services
- Analytics overview
- Profile and shop settings
- Upload a personal payment QR code
- Upload shop photos from multiple angles
- Separate phone number and email for each barber

## Roadmap
